Draft. This policy describes what the game really does today. It has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer, and the placeholders in [square brackets] must be filled in before launch.

The short version

  • We do not ask for your name, age, email address or any account. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • Your worlds and settings are saved on your own device. We never receive them.
  • The game has no adverts, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Multiplayer is optional. If you use it, the other players in your world can see your nickname and, because of how direct connections work, the network address of your device.

Who we are

Ard Blocks is run by [operator name], [country]. You can contact us at [contact email].

What is stored, and where

  • On your device: saved worlds, your settings (such as graphics and sound), progress and achievements, and a copy of the game's files so it can open without a connection. This lives in your browser's storage (or the desktop app's own storage). It stays on your device, and you can delete it by clearing this site's data or uninstalling the app.
  • Backups: if you use Export Backup, the file is saved where you choose. We do not receive it.
  • No cookies for advertising or tracking. The game uses browser storage only to make the game work.

Services that handle some data

  • Website hosting ([Cloudflare / GitHub Pages]). Like any website, the host receives your network address and basic request details (which file was requested, when, and your browser type) when the game loads, and may keep ordinary server logs for security and reliability. We do not use these to identify players.
  • Multiplayer matchmaking (PeerJS public server). When you host or join a world, your device contacts a free public matchmaking service to find the other player, using a short code. That service receives your network address and the code. After that, players connect directly to each other.
  • Other players. People in the same world can see your nickname, where you are in the world and, in worlds where typing is allowed, what you type. Because connections are direct, they may also learn your network address. We recommend joining only people you know.

Children

The game is written for children from about 8 and for families and classrooms. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, and we do not need any in order to play.

  • Family Mode is on by default: players cannot type their own messages, only choose from a short list of friendly Quick Chat phrases.
  • We suggest nicknames or first names only, never a full name, school or address.
  • Teachers can use Classroom Worlds, which switch student chat off by default and give the host controls.

If you believe a child has shared personal information in the game, or you have a concern about their safety, please write to [contact email] and we will respond as a priority.

Cost

The game is free. There are no payments, donations, ads or purchases of any kind, and nothing is locked behind a payment. If this ever changes, this page will say so first, and children will never be prompted to pay.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is normally nothing for us to give you, correct or delete. You can remove everything the game stores by clearing this site's data or uninstalling the app. If you think we hold something about you, contact [contact email]. Depending on where you live (for example under UK or EU data protection law), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, and to complain to your data protection authority.

Security

The game runs on your device and contains no accounts or passwords to steal. We check what other players send in multiplayer, limit how fast they can send it, and use a secure connection (HTTPS) to deliver the game. If we ever find a problem that affects players, we will fix it quickly and say so.

Changes

If we change what the game does with information, for example by adding accounts or cloud saves, we will update this page first and ask for a parent's permission where the law requires it. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

How to Play

Punch a tree for logs, make planks, a crafting table, then a wooden pickaxe. Stone and ore need the right pickaxe: iron ore needs stone, gold and diamond need iron.

Prayer: a day lasts 40 minutes. Before each prayer a villager climbs the minaret and calls the adhan, which you hear only near a masjid. The clock waits while the adhan is called. Villagers gather, and at the iqamah time the congregation prays together. Make wudu at any water, then press P to pray facing the qibla (shown top right); press P inside the masjid before the iqamah to join the congregation. Each prayer on time gives Iman +3, or +4 in congregation. Your wudu lasts one game day.

The journey: craft a compass (4 iron ingots around redstone dust, found deep underground), then follow it north through six stations. At each one a villager helps you get the next way to travel: a camel across the Great Desert, a boat across the sea and its gale, a flying carpet over the cliffs. In Madinah, earn the Ring of Trust and wake the flying machine, then fly through the storm to Makkah and perform Umrah. Stations are checkpoints: the game saves there, and if the heat or the sea overwhelms you, you wake at the last one. The panel on the right shows your next step.

Animals: sheep, cows and chickens live in green biomes and camels in the desert. Hold wheat (seeds for chickens) to lead them and feed two to raise a young one. Shear sheep for wool, which makes prayer rugs. Cook meat in a furnace.

Iman grows with dhikr, with Bismillah before eating and with returning a villager's salam. At 15 or more you have barakah: slower hunger and faster healing.
